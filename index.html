About

I am a mathematician working in elliptic partial differential equations, geometric analysis and geometric measure theory. I am currently a FAPESP postdoctoral fellow at UFABC, under the supervision of Stefano Nardulli. In 2025–2026 I spent a research year at the Università di Pisa, hosted by Aldo Pratelli, working on isoperimetric clusters on Riemannian and Finsler manifolds.

I received my PhD in Mathematics from the Universidade Federal da Paraíba (UFPB) in 2023, advised by João Marcos do Ó and co-advised by Guozhen Lu, including a visiting period at the University of Connecticut (2022–2023).

Research interests

  • Sharp Sobolev, Trudinger–Moser and Adams-type inequalities, including weighted Sobolev spaces and Riemannian manifolds
  • Symmetrization and Pólya–Szegő inequalities on manifolds and submanifolds
  • Variational and topological methods for the Allen–Cahn equation and systems
  • Isoperimetric problems and minimal clusters on Riemannian and Finsler manifolds
  • Elliptic PDEs, geometric analysis and geometric measure theory
